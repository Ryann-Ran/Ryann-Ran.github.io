I am Yuran Wang, a first-year master's student at Peking University.

From 2022 to 2025, I worked under the supervision of [Prof. Zheng Wang](https://wangzwhu.github.io/home/) at AIM Lab, Wuhan University.

Since 2024, I have been with DCAI Lab at Peking University under the supervision of [Prof. Wentao Zhang](https://zwt233.github.io/).

My research focuses on *visual generation* and *unified multimodal models*, aiming to build faithful, controllable, and creative generative intelligence for the real world. I am also interested in *world models*.
